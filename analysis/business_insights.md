# Sales Data Analysis – Business Insights

## 1. Sales Performance by Country

### Business Question
Which countries generate the highest total sales, and how many orders does each country have?

### Results

| Country | Total Sales | Order Count |
|---|---:|---:|
| Germany | 200 | 4 |
| USA | 180 | 6 |

### Key Findings

- Germany generated the highest total sales of 200 across 4 orders.
- USA generated total sales of 180 across 6 orders.
- Germany generated 20 more in total sales than the USA, despite having 2 fewer orders.
- Average sales per order were 50 in Germany and 30 in the USA.

### Business Interpretation

Germany had higher average sales per order, while the USA had a higher order volume.

This suggests an opportunity to investigate differences in order value and purchasing behavior across the two countries.

- INNER JOIN

- COUNT()
